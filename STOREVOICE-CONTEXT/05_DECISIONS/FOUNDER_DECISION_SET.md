## 6.1 Company Identity

* StoreVoice is a service company, not primarily a technology company.
* Voice AI is the means.
* The delivered value is the AI colleague and the service surrounding it.
* StoreVoice develops, produces, delivers, operates, supervises and improves AI colleagues.
* StoreVoice is intended to become a total-service provider for AI talent / AI colleagues.
* StoreVoice remains focused specifically on AI colleagues rather than becoming a generic AI consultancy.
* The customer buys a working service, not technology they must manage.

---

## 6.2 European Company

* StoreVoice is fundamentally Europe-oriented.
* StoreVoice intends to serve all European countries.
* Europe is treated as one strategic market.
* There is one central StoreVoice platform: `storevoice.ai`.
* StoreVoice does not create separate national StoreVoice products.
* Local sales teams may be created for expensive/premium packages.
* Commission-based salespeople may operate from anywhere.
* Local sales is a growth mechanism, not a prerequisite for entering a European market.
* After a sale, the customer relationship belongs to StoreVoice.
* Salespeople do not become permanent account managers merely because they made the sale.
* StoreVoice remains centrally controlled from Greece.
* StoreVoice is one Greek company rather than separate StoreVoice legal entities in each country.
* The intended legal model is operation from Greece under Greek law.

**IMPORTANT:** Do not independently determine whether this intended legal model is legally valid. That is a future legal/compliance validation question.

---

## 6.3 Central Platform

* `storevoice.ai` remains the central platform from which StoreVoice services are launched.
* Future specialized services or brands may exist if needed.
* Such extensions do not replace `storevoice.ai` as the central platform.
* The underlying product remains centrally controlled.

---

## 6.4 Local Customer Experience

StoreVoice must make the customer experience feel native to the customer's country.

Localization is NOT merely translation.

For a German customer, for example, the experience should feel German in:

* language
* wording
* communicative behavior
* cultural expression
* AI behavior
* customer-facing documentation
* invoices
* reports
* general service experience

The same principle applies across European countries.

The company's internal working language is English.

---

## 6.5 AI Colleague Philosophy

* AI is a colleague, not merely a tool.
* AI should feel natural, helpful and trustworthy.
* StoreVoice does not hide that the customer is interacting with AI.
* The AI may have a persona.
* Context and experience can develop that persona into a colleague-like experience.
* AI can recognize human emotion and respond appropriately.
* AI does not pretend to personally experience human emotions.
* AI may anticipate human needs but must not manufacture emotional situations.
* AI must not invent information.
* What is not known must not be invented.
* The AI operates within the approved knowledge, rules and permissions applicable to that customer.

---

## 6.6 Human Responsibility

* There is always human responsibility behind the AI colleague.
* This is a StoreVoice service promise.
* It is NOT currently intended as a separate contractual/legal clause.
* AI may operate autonomously within human-defined boundaries.
* Important decisions remain human decisions.
* Technology replacement remains a human decision.
* Important customer communications about technological changes are made by humans or initiated at human direction.
* Serious risks are escalated to humans.
* StoreVoice does not abandon customers when AI encounters problems.

---

## 6.7 Human Escalation

Human escalation is appropriate when:

* a decision is required
* emotion makes human involvement materially useful
* the caller explicitly requests a human
* the AI lacks required knowledge
* a human can materially help

For White Glove customers:

* human assistance is available 24/7
* a call can be transferred to a human
* outside human working hours, a callback can occur

Do not invent additional escalation rules.

---

## 6.8 Autonomous Operation

* AI should operate as autonomously as reasonably possible.
* Autonomous actions are allowed only where included in the customer's package and within StoreVoice rules.
* Technical capability does not automatically imply business authorization.
* Autonomy exists inside human-defined business, safety and compliance boundaries.

---

## 6.9 Technology Evolution

* StoreVoice should actively improve its technology.
* Better technology may replace existing technology.
* The decision to replace production technology is ALWAYS human.
* AI may test, compare and advise.
* AI may NOT autonomously decide to replace production technology.
* Existing customer continuity has priority over immediately adopting new technology.
* Significant technological changes may be communicated to customers when humans decide communication is appropriate.
* StoreVoice does not adopt technology merely because it is newer.

---

## 6.10 European Compliance

* StoreVoice intends to operate according to applicable European rules and regulations governing AI and its use.
* Compliance with European rules is a major differentiator.
* StoreVoice monitors relevant European AI regulatory developments.
* When applicable European rules change, StoreVoice adapts the service as necessary.
* Customers do not have an option to refuse changes that are necessary for regulatory compliance.
* If a capability may no longer legally be offered, StoreVoice removes it and provides a compliant alternative where possible.
* Compliance changes are centrally managed.
* The customer accepts the StoreVoice service under the European rules StoreVoice applies.

**IMPORTANT:** Do not invent legal requirements. Do not claim that a specific regulation requires a specific architecture. Legal interpretation belongs to a future compliance/legal workstream.

---

## 6.11 Customer Data and Information

* The customer decides what business information and data to provide.
* StoreVoice checks whether the intended use fits applicable European rules.
* Information that cannot legitimately be used is not accepted for the AI colleague.
* StoreVoice explains what needs to change.
* The customer remains responsible for the correctness and legality of information supplied by the customer.
* StoreVoice remains responsible for its processing within the StoreVoice service.
* StoreVoice proactively warns about potential compliance, privacy or safety risks.
* Potential risks are escalated to a human.

---

## 6.12 Conflicting Information

When customer information conflicts:

* StoreVoice does not allow the AI to arbitrarily select the truth.
* The conflict is presented to the customer.
* The customer decides which information is correct.
* The last approved information remains authoritative until the customer confirms the new information.
* The AI continues operating using safe, approved information while the conflict is unresolved.

---

## 6.13 Knowledge Transparency

* The customer can see the information currently approved as knowledge for the AI colleague.
* The customer can correct it.
* Changes, blocks and replacements are recorded.
* Knowledge history functions as an audit trail.
* StoreVoice must be able to reconstruct relevant information and context during an incident.
* The complete technical audit trail is initially managed by StoreVoice.
* StoreVoice determines which relevant portions are shared with customers.

---

## 6.14 Incident Management

* StoreVoice takes human responsibility for serious customer problems.
* StoreVoice proactively investigates problems that could affect multiple customers.
* Safety and continuity come before complete root-cause analysis.
* In exceptional circumstances, an AI colleague may be taken offline to protect customers.
* StoreVoice should provide an alternative wherever possible.
* Every serious incident receives a human post-incident review.
* Post-incident review may result in structural product or operational changes.
* Approved structural improvements are centrally implemented for relevant customers.

---

## 6.15 Central Product Standard

* StoreVoice maintains one central product standard.
* Product improvements generally apply to all relevant customers.
* Local or customer-specific deviations are exceptions.
* Every deviation must be explicitly recorded.
* The reason for the deviation must be recorded.
* Human approval of the deviation must be recorded.
* Long-term technical divergence between customers should be avoided.
* Customer-specific development should only be pursued when it can ultimately become useful to the general product.
* StoreVoice can say no to work that conflicts with the vision or central product standard, regardless of financial value.

---

## 6.16 Customer Fit

* StoreVoice does NOT establish an automatic customer-selection mechanism as part of the business vision.
* Do not invent customer exclusion criteria.

---

## 6.17 Multi-Tenant Principle

* StoreVoice is always multi-tenant.
* Each customer has a strictly separated account/tenant.
* Customer data, context, configuration and knowledge must not cross tenant boundaries.
* Central infrastructure may be shared, but customer information remains separated.

**IMPORTANT:** Do not yet choose the technical implementation of tenant isolation.

---

## 6.18 Learning Across Customers

* StoreVoice may learn from general operational experience.
* StoreVoice may use anonymized and aggregated patterns to improve the general product.
* Individual customer data must not become another customer's AI knowledge.
* Confidential customer information must not flow between customers.
* StoreVoice may retain non-identifiable general insights after a customer leaves.
* StoreVoice does not sell or rent customer data.
* StoreVoice does not provide third parties with commercial insights about individual customers.

---

## 6.19 Ownership

Customer owns:

* the business information the customer provides.

StoreVoice owns:

* its technology
* its platform
* its general methodologies
* general operational knowledge
* QA knowledge
* general improvements
* general best practices arising from the company as a whole

General improvements derived from anonymized/aggregated customer insights belong to StoreVoice.

---

## 6.20 Customer Exit

The previously approved customer lifecycle remains authoritative:

* Services are prepaid.
* Standard packages are monthly cancellable.
* Cancellation stops renewal.
* Service continues through the paid period.
* The account has a 14-day recovery period.
* The customer can reactivate during those 14 days.
* After 14 days, customer-specific data and AI knowledge are permanently deleted, subject to legally required retention.
* A returning customer after permanent deletion is treated as a new customer.
* Old knowledge, settings and conversations are not automatically restored.
* StoreVoice is not required to provide an export of accumulated AI knowledge after termination.

---

## 6.21 Central Customer Administration

* StoreVoice operates one central European customer administration from Greece.
* There are not separate country-specific customer administrations.
* Customer-facing administration should nevertheless be localized to the customer's country where already decided.

* Features are tied to packages.
* Customers cannot arbitrarily bolt on individual features outside their package.
* Upgrade is immediate and prorated through Stripe.
* Downgrade takes effect at the end of the current month.
* Customers can monthly upgrade or downgrade.

## 6.26 Phone

Previously established:

* StoreVoice uses Twilio for phone service.

* Local number provisioning is intended to be automatic.
* Where local telecom rules or number availability make this difficult, an alternative is offered.

## 6.27 Onboarding

* White Glove onboarding is human-led.
* StoreVoice employees interview the customer and collect relevant information/documents.
* StoreVoice configures, trains/instructs, tests and refines the AI colleague.
* Target is approximately 5–7 days, but quality is the readiness gate.
* A/B onboarding can in principle be fully AI-driven based on documents and information.
* Customers can always add or correct relevant information.
* There is no fixed cap on relevant customer information.
* Onboarding progress should be visible to the customer.
* 100% means ready to go live.
* There is no separate mandatory manual testing gate after onboarding reaches 100%.
* AI should be able to update from relevant customer interactions/information within its permitted boundaries.
* QA/reporting identifies knowledge gaps, contradictions, recurring questions, escalations and patterns.
* AI may in principle modify itself only within business rules, safety and compliance boundaries.

Do not convert these into technical implementation decisions yet.

---

## 6.28 White Glove

White Glove means StoreVoice takes responsibility for virtually everything required to operate the AI colleague while the subscription remains active.

This includes, where applicable:

* onboarding
* knowledge collection
* training/instruction
* testing
* QA
* operations
* reporting
* improvements
* knowledge updates
* human escalation

White Glove is available as a service option for A/B.

Enterprise is inherently high-service.

---

## 6.29 Demo

The existing approved demo concept remains authoritative:

* Prospect enters the website.
* StoreVoice uses the prospect's website information to create a temporary demo agent.
* The demo uses only scraped website knowledge.
* It is a commercial experience, not a full trial.
* The intended experience is approximately 90 seconds.
* Demo agent can introduce itself as the company's AI assistant/colleague.
* The demo should feel like the future StoreVoice service rather than a technical demonstration.
* Demo may support sharing with a colleague/partner.
* Follow-up includes immediate/near-immediate SMS and email sequencing as previously defined.
* Demo expires after approximately 48 hours if no action is taken.

Do not invent additional demo mechanics.
